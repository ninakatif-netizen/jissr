Closing the screening-to-care loop

Every abnormal result, all the way to care.

A screening result only helps if someone acts on it. Falaj gives every abnormal result an owner and a deadline in seconds, reaches the patient in their own language, and follows them across clinics with one Health ID, until a doctor confirms care.

Why it works

Reminders alone are not enough; reaching people is. Falaj is built on what trials show.

Follow-up after an abnormal screening result, at 120 days

Usual care22.9%
Reminders only22.7%
Outreach + navigation31.4%
Atlas et al., JAMA 2023

Usual care42.1%
