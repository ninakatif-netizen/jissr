Falaj · for nurses and doctors

See where every abnormal result is, and where the water stops.

Falaj shows a care team every patient with an abnormal screening result as water flowing to care: reached, understood, booked, seen, referred, closed. You see who is stuck and the one thing to do. Agents call, remind and chase; people decide.

1

See the flow

Every patient from result to loop closed, channel by channel. Tap a channel: who stopped there, and since when.

2

One action per patient

Call, book, refer, accept, confirm. Each person sees only what needs them, most urgent first.

3

Agents that never decide

They call in the patient's language, remind and chase referrals. They never give a result or close a loop.
