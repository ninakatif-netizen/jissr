Why it works

Reminders alone are not enough; reaching people is. Falaj is built on what trials show.

23% → 31%

Follow-up after an abnormal screening result rose when patients were actively reached, while reminders alone did no better than usual care (22.7% vs 22.9%).

Atlas et al., JAMA 2023, 120 days
42.1% → 55.1%

Colonoscopy after an abnormal bowel test rose with phone navigation in the patient's own language.

Coronado et al., Annals of Internal Medicine 2025

Falaj itself has not been measured yet: that is what a pilot is for. Everything for the jury

Falaj (فلج) is the water channel that has fed the oases of the Emirates for centuries: every plot, a named keeper, a known turn. Falaj does the same for care.
