Path to pilot

From prototype to a measured pilot in 90 days.

One clinic, one programme (abnormal HbA1c), run next to the current process. We measure the same things before and during the pilot, then decide together whether to extend it.

Weeks 1–3

Prepare

  • Name a clinical lead; sign off thresholds, deadlines and messages
  • Native Arabic review of every patient message
  • Data protection impact assessment; DoH approvals and ethics as required
  • Connect the lab result feed (FHIR); test with Malaffi where possible
  • Measure the baseline from the last months of the current process
  • Train the nurse, coordinator and clinicians (one hour)
Weeks 4–10

Run

  • Every new actionable HbA1c result enters Jissr automatically
  • Patients get their result in plain Arabic or English, with consent
  • The care team works from "My tasks" and the alerts queue
  • Weekly 15-minute review of alerts and any safety concern
  • AI wording only after clinical sign-off; templates otherwise
Weeks 11–13

Measure and decide

  • Compare every measure with the baseline
  • Interview patients and staff
  • Safety report: every escalation and how it was resolved
  • Decide: stop, adjust, extend to more clinics, or add a programme (for example bowel screening)

What a pilot partner provides

  • A clinical lead (about 2 hours a week)
  • One nurse and one coordinator using Jissr in their normal work
  • Access to the lab result feed for one programme
  • Help with approvals, consent wording and the security review
  • We provide: the software, set-up, training, weekly support
  • Hosting planned in a UAE cloud region, so data stays in the country
  • A full evaluation report at week 13
  • Everything already built: patient app, care team, rules, measures, safety tests

Deployment architecture

Today the prototype runs in the browser with synthetic data. For a pilot, the same rules engine moves behind a secure service hosted in the UAE.

Business model (proposal)

Who benefits and who pays

Provider networks and payers: fewer people lost after screening, earlier care, quality measures they can report. Health authority: one view of whether screening programmes lead to care.

Pricing options to agree with a partner: an annual licence per screening programme, or a fee per actionable result managed. No price is claimed here.

Why it can scale cheaply

One engine runs every programme: adding one means adding its content (thresholds, messages, referral places), not new software. Running costs are hosting, messages (SMS or WhatsApp) and short AI wording requests. The main cost is set-up and training per site.
