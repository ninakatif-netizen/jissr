Clinical review sheet

Please review Jissr's clinical rules and messages

Jissr is a prototype for the Future Health Challenge 2026 ("Closing the Screening-to-Care Loop"), covering one programme: abnormal HbA1c from screening. All patients in the demo are invented. The numbers and wording below were written by a medical student with software help and have not been clinically reviewed. Your review decides what we can honestly say about them.

What we ask: for each line, tick Agree or write the change you would make. About 20 minutes. You are not asked to endorse the product, only to correct the content. Where a local guideline applies (for example DoH Abu Dhabi), please say which.
