# Falaj · 3-minute pitch

Future Health Challenge 2026, challenge 05: closing the screening-to-care loop.
Live demo: https://jissr-three.vercel.app (start with "Take the 3-minute guided tour").

## The script (about 3 minutes)

**0:00 · The problem (20 s)**
"A screening result only saves a life if it reaches care. In real life it crosses a lab, a programme,
a family doctor, a hospital, and sometimes an insurer. At every handover it can stop, and nobody notices."

**0:20 · The idea (20 s)**
"For 3,000 years the aflaj carried water from a mother well to every palm garden of the oases.
Each channel had a keeper, the arif, and every plot a fair turn. Falaj does the same for a result:
it flows to care, a named person keeps it moving, and any leak is seen."

**0:40 · Priya, the patient (50 s)**
Guided tour, steps 1 and 2.
"Priya, 47, speaks Malayalam. Her mammogram is BI-RADS 4. Falaj tells her in plain words that this is
not a diagnosis of cancer. She explains it back in her own words, and books a 10-minute call with a nurse.
Her first message never contains the result."

**1:30 · The care team (40 s)**
Steps 3 to 5.
"The nurse sees who to call first. A suspicious mammogram is marked 'call required': told by a person,
never by a message alone. The dropout radar shows why Priya might be lost: her language, a result not opened.
The referral is a handshake: the hospital must take it, or the sender stays responsible, and insurance
approval is requested at the same time. A 2-minute consult often makes the referral unnecessary."

**2:10 · The system (30 s)**
Step 6, the living map.
"For the Department of Health: where does the water stop? Open and late cases in every emirate, the biggest
leak, who holds the water, and who drops out by language. Every number is computed from the cases."

**2:40 · Trust (20 s)**
Steps 7 and 8.
"When care is in place, a clinician closes the case with evidence, and Priya keeps a proof of care.
And you can try to break it: the AI cannot close a case, skip a step or remove an owner."

**3:00 · Close (10 s)**
"Falaj: no abnormal result left behind."

## Honest answers to expected questions

- **Is the data real?** No. Synthetic cases only; every source is declared in DATA-SOURCES.md.
- **Are the clinical rules validated?** Proposed by the team's medical student; awaiting sign-off by a
  supervising clinician (review.html, section 7).
- **Integrations?** Simulated: Malaffi / NABIDH / Riayati, UAE PASS, insurers. The pilot plan (pilot.html)
  shows how they would connect.
- **Translations?** The first message exists in 7 languages as drafts for medical translators.
- **AI?** It only drafts wording. Fixed rules decide urgency, owners, deadlines and closure.
