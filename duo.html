The Health ID, live

Two phones, one screen.

Priya's phone is on the left, Dr Mariam's on the right. They are both real and talk to each other, so you can watch the key work.

  1. Read Priya's codeSix digits under her QR code. It changes every minute.
  2. Type it on the doctor's phoneHer ID and name are filled in. Add the live code, then "Open care map".
  3. Watch both phonesThe map opens on the right. Priya's phone says who opened her file.
  4. Take the key backOn Priya's phone, tap "Remove access": the doctor's map closes.
